### Problem
Fleet operations involve multiple responsibilities, from vehicle and driver management to trip coordination, maintenance, and financial tracking. Managing these workflows across different roles requires a centralized system with controlled access and reliable data management.

### Built
Developed a full-stack fleet management application with JWT authentication and role-based access for four user roles. Incorporated input validation, password hashing, database transactions, and a modular backend architecture.

### Outcome
∴ Delivered a centralized fleet management platform with role-specific access and integrated operational workflows as part of the Odoo Hackathon.

## DocSaathi
- slug: docsaathi
- tagline: A privacy-first, local AI document assistant that helps users understand English documents in their preferred language.
- year: 2026
- status: completed
- role: Hackathon Project | AI & Backend Integration
- stack: Gemma 4, Ollama, React , Express , Node , Local LLM Inference 
- repo: https://github.com/Navikeshsaravanan/DocSaathi-AI
- live: 
- image:/project/Doc-saathi.png
- xray:

### Problem
Understanding complex English documents can be difficult for people who are more comfortable with regional languages. Uploading sensitive documents to cloud-based AI services also raises privacy concerns, while AI-generated explanations can sometimes misinterpret important facts such as dates and amounts.

### Built
Contributed to a privacy-first document assistant powered by Gemma 4 and Ollama, enabling local document processing and multilingual explanations across nine languages. Worked on the initial Ollama and backend integration, port configuration, bug fixes, and improving explanation quality. Implemented a fact-verification approach where deterministic code validates important details such as dates, amounts, and source quotes rather than relying entirely on model-generated responses.

### Outcome
∴ Built a local-first AI document assistant that combines multilingual document understanding with fact verification, keeping sensitive document processing on the user's machine.
